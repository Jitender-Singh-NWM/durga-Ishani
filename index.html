A DUSSEHRA OFFERINGJai Mata Di

A moment of devotion

Enter the temple to begin your offering
ॐ

YOU ARE WARMLY INVITED

A moment with
Durga Mata.

Ring the bell. Make an offering.
Let your heart be filled with light.

With warm wishes from Ishani

YOUR VIRTUAL PUJA

May goodness triumph, today and always.

ॐ

WITH MAA DURGA’S BLESSINGS

May your life be filled
with light.

May Maa Durga bless you and your family with courage, peace, good health, and happiness.

✦ ✦ ✦

Ishani

wishes you

Happy Dussehra
